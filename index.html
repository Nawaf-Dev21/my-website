ABOUT

A few hundred players never see this part of the server

I'm Nawaf, known in the FiveM community as Abo3tb. Since 2023 I've been building backend systems for GTA roleplay servers — the parts that keep a session stable when the player count spikes, not the parts that show up in a trailer.

Most of my work sits on QBCore and its QB-Box branch: custom resources, database structures, and the server-side logic that decides whether a job system, an inventory, or a banking script survives contact with real players.

Backend is where most of my time goes, and it's what I'd point you to first. I also build and adjust the frontend side of these systems when needed — NUI menus, HUD elements, admin dashboards — comfortably, though it's not where I spend the bulk of my hours.
